change, schema migration, live fleet replay or model expenditure. The working baseline already
contains #463 (absolute-only explicit Action destinations), #465 (bounded MIT account handles)
and #471 (all unresolved hypotheses retained). Their behavior remains covered by existing tests.

An analyst selection or recorded PASS remains an attestation. It is not independent semantic
judgment, provider authentication, human acceptance or default-branch ancestry. Common source
review and per-arm generation stay separate; missing outcomes/model costs are unknown.

Final review controls also refuse evicted digest-page continuations, branch/detached transitions
and edits during snapshot validation. Explore init cannot relabel a stale comparison with a new
receiving ref or policy. Conflicting selections make the selected-change count unknown and expose a conflict
count; a later unresolved judgement cannot inherit readiness from a registration. Paired imports
check effective digest-input fingerprints and explicit branch/detached assertions, rather than
merely storing declared coverage/budget metadata beside matching commits.

The broader #208 invalidation redesign, #326 persistent shallow caching, #328 deep-menu support,
#228 CLI audit, #200/#303 ranking acceptance and #250/#256 campaigns remain open. Finishing a
selected slice must not close its entire historical issue. Validation uses local authored
fixtures plus the repository's existing platform/install/acquisition checks on the PR candidate.
